> **This is not the official Rats mod.** It is an unofficial port to Minecraft 26.3, made with the help of AI. The
> Rats team didn't make, check or support it. It is published with the Rats maintainer's permission, only for
> Minecraft versions the official mod doesn't support. If an official build exists for your version, use that one.
>
> Please report problems with this port on [its GitHub issues](https://github.com/Panda-s-Workshop/rats-unofficial/issues),
> not to the Rats team.

[Rats](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/rats) by AlexThe666 and GizmoTheMoonPig was last released for
Minecraft 1.20.1. This port brings it to **Minecraft 26.3 on NeoForge**.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.3 and NeoForge 26.3.0.89 or newer, Java 25.
- Nothing else. Citadel, which Rats needs, has no public 26.3 build, so its 26.3 port is bundled inside this jar. A
  newer Citadel in your mods folder takes its place.

## What's in it

- Wild rats, and tamed rats that you dress, feed and command.
- Rat upgrades that give a rat a job: farming, mining, fishing, fighting, carrying and more.
- The Cheese Staff, for telling a rat where to pick up and where to drop off.
- Rat Tubes for rats to travel through.
- The Plague, plague doctors and the Black Death, the Rat King and the Pied Piper.
- Ratlantis, its mobs, bosses and gear (on by default in new worlds; the config can turn it off).

## Known gaps

- The Cheese Staff's and radius staff's in-world overlays don't draw.
- About 35 items that used to glow (ratbow essences, vials, upgrade cores) draw without their glow.
- Ratlantis has no day and night fog tint.
- JEI and Jade support isn't ported yet.

## Made with AI

Most of the porting work, from the 1.21.1 community fixes through JakeMalmrose's 26.1 port to these 26.3 changes, was
written with AI and checked by people playing it. The full history and every change are in the
[source](https://github.com/Panda-s-Workshop/rats-unofficial).
